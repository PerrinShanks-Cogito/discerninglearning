Reading these numbers
  • Check it's England. Most "NHS" figures cover England only. Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland publish their own.
  • Check the period. Budgets are for a financial year, costs for the year the collection covers, and waiting times for a single month. They don't all line up.
  • Guy's and St Thomas' is not an average hospital. It runs specialist services most trusts don't, so the cost index and the Shelford comparison are fairer than the raw figures.

This is my personal site, so these are my reading of public data, not an official view of the NHS or of Guy's and St Thomas' NHS Foundation Trust.
