Where these figures come from

The distances and the speed of light come from the sources linked on each card. Everything else is arithmetic: a year is taken as 365.25 days, a football pitch as 105 by 68 metres (the size used at most major grounds) and an Olympic pool at its minimum depth of 2 metres. These figures don't change, so this page isn't part of the quarterly check.
