The idea comes from Tim Harford's How to Make the World Add Up (published in the US as The Data Detective). Harford suggests carrying a handful of landmark numbers in your head, such as the population of the country or what the government spends in a year. When a new statistic comes along, you can check it against a landmark and tell whether it is big, small or simply implausible. The credit for the idea is his. Any mistakes in the numbers are mine.
