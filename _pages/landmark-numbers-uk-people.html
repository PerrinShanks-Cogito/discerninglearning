Notes on these figures
  • "Working age" here means 16 to 64, the standard statistical definition. The State Pension age is now 66, and plenty of people work beyond it or don't work before it, so it's a measure of age, not of jobs. Household figures come from the Labour Force Survey, which no longer covers Northern Ireland.
  • Migration figures come in two versions. The table uses the mid-year population estimates, which split migration by nation. The UK headline uses the ONS's later, provisional migration estimates, which are revised as more data arrives. "Migration within the UK" is people moving between the four nations.
  • Healthy life expectancy is based on how people rate their own health, so it is less precise than life expectancy, especially for the smaller nations.
  • Religion comes from each nation's census, taken in 2021 (Scotland 2022), so it changes only with a new census.
